Getting started

How it works

  1. Sign in with Rice

    Use your @rice.edu Google account. Personal accounts are not accepted.

  2. Pick a session

    Each session shows its instructor, location and how many seats are left.

  3. Get your invitation

    You are added straight to the shared course calendar and receive a confirmation email. Accept the calendar invite so you get the reminder.

  4. Cancel if plans change

    Release your seat from My Bookings so a classmate can take it. Your instructor is notified automatically.

Before you come to the OEDK

Closed-toe shoes are required in all fabrication areas. Arrive a few minutes early - safety briefings start on time, and a missed briefing means a missed session. If you can no longer attend, cancel rather than not showing up: seats are limited and these sessions fill.
