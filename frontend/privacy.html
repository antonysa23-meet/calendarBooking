Policy

Privacy

This site is run by the teaching staff of EDES 210 and BIOE 555 at Rice University to manage bookings for equipment and training sessions at the Oshman Engineering Design Kitchen. It is a course tool, not a commercial service.

Last updated 25 August 2026.

What we collect

When you sign in with your Rice Google account, Google gives this site your name, email address and profile picture. We use the email address to confirm you are a member of the Rice community and to identify your bookings.

When you book a session, we record your name, email address, which session you booked and the time you booked it. If you cancel, we record that too, along with when.

Your profile picture is shown in the page header while you are signed in. It is never stored - it is read from Google each time and disappears when you sign out.

We do not use cookies for tracking, we run no analytics, and there are no advertising or third-party scripts on this site.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm that you hold a Rice University account. Only @rice.edu accounts can sign in.
  • To keep sessions within their seat limit and stop the same person booking a session twice.
  • To add you as a guest on the session's Google Calendar event, so you get the invitation and reminder.
  • To email you a booking confirmation, and to tell the session's instructor who is coming.
  • To give instructors a roster, so they know who to expect and can follow up about attendance or safety requirements.

Where it is stored

All of it lives inside Google Workspace, in accounts owned by the course:

  • Bookings and session details are in a Google Sheet owned by the course account.
  • Sessions are Google Calendar events on a calendar owned by the same account. Booking a session adds you as a guest on that event.
  • Confirmation emails are sent through Gmail from that account.

Nothing is stored on any other server, and nothing is shared with anyone outside the course. Your data is never sold, and it is never used to train any model.

One thing worth knowing about calendar invitations: when you book a session you become a guest on that calendar event, alongside the instructor and the other students who booked it. By default Google Calendar lets guests on an event see the other guests' names and email addresses. This is standard Google Calendar behaviour and is the same as being invited to any shared event.

Your sign-in

Signing in produces a short-lived Google identity token. It is held in your browser's session storage, is discarded when you close the tab or sign out, and expires within an hour regardless. The site never sees or stores your password, and it has no access to your Google account beyond your name, email address and profile picture.

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for the academic year so instructors can confirm who completed which training, then cleared out. Cancelled bookings are marked cancelled rather than deleted, so that the record of who attended what stays accurate.

Seeing or removing your data

You can see all of your own bookings at any time on the My Bookings page, and cancel any of them yourself.

To ask what is held about you, to correct it, or to have it removed, contact the EDES 210 / BIOE 555 teaching staff - the instructor named on any session you booked, or the course email address. Bear in mind that removing a booking record also removes the evidence that you completed a required safety training.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
